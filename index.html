Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding OPD consultations, emergency admissions, insurance, and reports.

How can I book an appointment with a specialist doctor at KLE Hospital?

You can book directly through our online appointment system on this website, call our central appointment desk at +91 831 247 1000, or walk in directly to our outpatient registration desks between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM.

Is emergency trauma care and ambulance assistance available 24/7?

Yes, our Level-1 Trauma Centre and Advanced Life Support (ALS) Mobile ICU ambulances operate round-the-clock, 365 days a year. Call our direct Emergency Hotline at +91 831 247 3777 or +91 831 247 9999 for instant response.

How does cashless insurance hospitalization work at KLE Hospital?

Our dedicated 24/7 TPA & Insurance Desk is located on the ground floor. Present your health insurance card, government ID, and policy number. Our team coordinates pre-authorization with over 40+ insurance companies and TPAs for a cashless admission.

Can I view and download my laboratory reports online?

Yes! Use our 'Patient Portal / Lab Reports' feature on this website. Enter your unique Patient ID (e.g. KLE-1001) or registered mobile number to immediately view, print, or download your verified diagnostic reports.

What are the visiting hours for inpatients and intensive care units?

General Wards: 04:30 PM - 07:00 PM daily. ICUs & Critical Care Units: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM & 05:00 PM - 06:00 PM (restricted to 1 attendee at a time with sanitization protocols).
